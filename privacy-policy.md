# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 29, 2026**

JANIJU STUDIO ("we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile game **QuizRun: Dual Gate** (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you use the Game, including information processed through third-party services integrated into the Game.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Process

### 1.1 Google Play Games Information

The Game may use **Google Play Games Services** to provide features such as player sign-in and leaderboards.

If you choose to use these features, Google Play Games Services may provide or process certain player information, such as your player name, player ID, profile information, and leaderboard-related data, depending on your Google account settings and the services you use.

We use this information only as necessary to provide and maintain the relevant game features.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

### 1.2 Game Data

The Game may generate and store gameplay-related information, including:

- Scores and high scores
- Gameplay statistics
- Game progress
- Game settings and preferences, such as sound, music, and control settings

Some of this information may be stored locally on your device.

If you use leaderboard features, certain gameplay information, such as your leaderboard score, may be submitted to and processed by Google Play Games Services.

### 1.3 Advertising Information

The Game uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and related Google advertising technologies may automatically collect or process certain information, which may include:

- Device or other identifiers
- Advertising identifiers, where available
- IP address
- App interactions and usage information
- Advertising interaction and performance information
- Diagnostic and technical information

The specific information collected or processed may vary depending on your device, operating system, region, privacy choices, and applicable law.

We do not directly control the information collected or processed by Google AdMob.

Where required by applicable law, you may be presented with options regarding personalized advertising, consent, or other privacy choices.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 1.4 Analytics Information

The Game uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how the Game is played — for example, which modes are played, how far players progress, whether the tutorial is completed, and whether optional features such as reviving or sharing are used.

Google Analytics for Firebase may collect or process certain information, which may include:

- App interactions and in-game events (for example, starting and finishing a run, the score reached, and tutorial completion)
- An app instance identifier
- Device information, such as device model and operating system version
- Approximate location derived from the IP address

Analytics is turned on only in regions where consent is not required. For players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, analytics collection stays turned off.

We use this information in aggregate to improve the Game, such as its design and difficulty. We do not use it to identify individual players.

For more information, please review Firebase's privacy information:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 1.5 In-App Purchase Information

The Game may offer in-app purchases, including an option to remove advertisements.

Purchases are processed through **Google Play Billing**.

We do not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information. Payment information and transactions are processed by Google in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

We may receive or process limited purchase-related information necessary to verify purchases, restore purchased features, prevent fraud, and provide purchased content or functionality.

### 1.6 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

We do not intentionally request or directly collect through the Game information such as:

- Your home address
- Your phone number
- Your contacts
- Photos, videos, or camera content
- Microphone recordings
- Precise location

However, third-party services integrated into the Game may process certain technical, device, advertising, or usage information as described in this Privacy Policy and in their own privacy policies.
